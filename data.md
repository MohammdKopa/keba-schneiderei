## Design Direction

- Editorial / Savile Row tailor house aesthetic
- Monochrome palette: deep charcoal `#0F0F10`, ivory `#F5F1EA`, aged brass `#B08D57`, thread red `#7A1E1E`
- Typography: **Cormorant Garamond** (display) + **Inter** (body) + **Noto Naskh Arabic** (Arabic)
- Motifs: stitched-line SVG dividers, measuring-tape markers, thread textures
- Motion: GSAP split-text hero, pinned scroll craft section, parallax atelier, horizontal-scroll gallery, magnetic CTAs
- Accessibility: `prefers-reduced-motion` honored, full keyboard nav, ARIA labels on lang toggle
